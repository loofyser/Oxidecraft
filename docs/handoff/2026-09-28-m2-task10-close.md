## The pass's settled shape (Task 11 extends it)

The pass owns one pipeline per layer over the shared 28-byte vertex layout (position `Float32x3` at
0, uv `Float32x2` at 12, light `Uint16x2` at 20, colour `Unorm8x4` at 24) and the shared depth test
(`Depth32Float`, `Less`). The per-layer differences are: the fragment entry (`fs_main` for Opaque;
`fs_cutout`, discarding `alpha < 0.1`, for Cutout and Translucent — the client's alpha test is in
force at all three of its block-layer draws), the cull mode (back faces for all three, the
translucent layer included), the blend (`None`, `None`, and for Translucent colour
`SrcAlpha/OneMinusSrcAlpha` with the separate alpha pair `One/Zero`), and the depth write (off for
Translucent only). Group 0 is the camera uniform (vertex-visible); group 1 is the atlas texture and
sampler (fragment-visible). `set_atlas` (uploads once; a second call replaces), `set_camera(&mut
self, …)` (stores the `Frustum` and the eye for the next `draw`), `set_section_mesh` (per-layer
upload; a `None` or an empty mesh removes, and empty layers are freed) and `draw` (no atlas or no
camera means no draws; per-section culling before each layer's draws; Translucent last, sections in
descending eye-to-centre distance) keep their call shapes. The atlas uploader's guards (clamp
`level_count` to the texture's mip limit; skip a mismatched level, left zero-filled) are documented
and never trip on a stitcher-built atlas.

Declared divergences in view (plan and ledger): the atlas sampler clamps `ClampToEdge` where the
client leaves the block atlas at `GL_REPEAT` — unobservable for stitcher-built content-rect uvs
except an exact-1.0 sample on a flush cell; the translucent order is per section, not per surface
(the plan's known limit 4); the depth compare is `Less` where the client sets `GL_LEQUAL`
(pre-existing M1; ledgered for the final review).

Interim state: the fragment stage now samples the atlas and multiplies the vertex colour; the
lightmap sample, the brightness and sun path, the colour-space policy and fog are Task 11's, over
this same fragment path and the pass's frame state. The shipping client still meshes the fallback
and logs once until Task 14's bootstrap (a session with no atlas draws nothing).

## How the work runs

One task at a time, in plan order. For each task: extract its text from the plan into
`task-N-brief.md`, carry it out following the plan's steps and the Global Constraints, record the
report as `task-N-report.md`, freeze the task's diff, have that diff reviewed independently as
`task-N-review.md`, fix and re-review when findings require it, and append one line per event to the
ledger. All of it lives in the local scratch space (git-ignored). The ledger is the recovery map: after any interruption, trust the ledger and
`git log` over recollection, and never redo a task it marks complete.
