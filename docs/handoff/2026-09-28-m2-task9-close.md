## The layers' settled shape (Task 10 extends it)

`build_section_mesh(snapshot, sy, ctx)` and `build_column_meshes(snapshot, ctx)` keep their shapes.
`ChunkMesh` is now layer-keyed: `Layer::ALL` in order Opaque, Cutout, Translucent; one `LayerMesh`
per layer with `vertices` and `indices`; `vertex_count()` kept as the sum across layers (the
`terrain_pass.rs` call site wants the total); `is_empty()` reads the layers' indices. The translucent
layer's quads are stably sorted once in the builder — descending distance from the section centre
`(8.0, sy * 16 + 8.0, 8.0)` to the mean of each quad's four vertex positions — so a section's water
draws back to front from outside; per-frame camera ordering is the renderer's job. `terrain_pass.rs`
today walks the layers into the single M1 buffer with index rebasing (the interim shape adaptation);
Task 10 replaces that with one pipeline per layer, the per-layer upload, per-layer removal, and
per-section frustum culling in front of the draws.

The liquid rules the mesher holds are derived in `task-9-report.md` §Derivation (`File.java:line`
each): the top surface at the corner-averaged `getFluidHeight` heights with the 0.001 sink; sides
from the block's base to those heights, each side emitted twice; the bottom face; the second top
pass gated by `shouldRenderSides(pos.up())`; the same-material cull with the up face kept under any
non-liquid neighbour; the light as the per-channel max of the cell and the cell above (the bottom
face samples the cell **below** — the source's `blockPosIn.down()`); the water tint through the
nine-sample helper; the untinted 0.5-grey bottom; the still/flowing sprites with the top's
flow-angle uv rotation. The ring predicate reads the construction-time `fullBlock` (`Block.java:295`)
through the table's `occludes` column — not `full_cube`, and not the graphics-overridden value (a
fix-round adjudication; `task-9-review.md` F1 and the report's `## Fix round 1`).

Known interim state: the fragment stage is still untextured until Tasks 10–11; every layer currently
draws through the single concatenated buffer with no blending and the one pipeline state (Task 10's

## How the work runs

`task-N-brief.md`, carry it out following the plan's steps and the Global Constraints, record the
report as `task-N-report.md`, freeze the task's diff, have that diff reviewed independently as
`task-N-review.md`, fix and re-review when findings require it, and append one line per event to the
ledger. All of it lives in the local scratch space (git-ignored). The ledger is the recovery map: after any interruption, trust the ledger and
`git log` over recollection, and never redo a task it marks complete.
