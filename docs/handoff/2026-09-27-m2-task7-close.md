## The light rule that was settled (Task 8 consumes it per vertex)

Light entering a cell loses the receiving cell's light opacity: 1 through a transparent cell, 3 into
water or ice, 1 into leaves or cobwebs, nothing into an opaque non-emitter, and 1 into an opaque
emitter (the source's opaque-and-luminous clause). Sky light is 15 strictly above a column's topmost
non-transparent cell; everything else spreads by the same recurrence. `light_at(world, x, y, z)`
answers `max(sky, block)` and 0 outside the loaded columns; `recompute`/`recompute_column` are the
entry points; a section update stores the payload's light with no relight (the block-change path is
M3's). Spec §9, report §4.1 and the plan's Task 7 text carry the "corrected after execution"
annotations. The rule's proof against the source is in the ledger's Task 7 block.
