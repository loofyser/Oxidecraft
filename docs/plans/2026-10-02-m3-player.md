# M3 Player Implementation Plan

> **How to work this plan:** one task at a time, in order. Run each step's verification before moving on, and tick the checkboxes (`- [ ]`) as you go. Every commit is made on `main` with explicit `git add` paths.
>
> **Plan style:** this plan pins every interface, constant, path, and rule the tasks must agree on, and carries code sketches wherever a byte layout, formula, or data shape is the deliverable. Algorithm bodies that mirror vanilla behaviour are derived from the cited MCP-919 source files (`refs/_src/MCP-919`, local and uncommitted; the tree is present from the previous milestone, HEAD `1717f75902c6184a1ed1bfcd7880404aab4da503`) by the task that needs them, and every derived constant is pinned by a test. Where this plan and a cited document disagree, the document governs and the plan text is corrected first. Pin the source's expression and cite the method that applies it where the two differ — a factor can live in the applier, not the computer.

**Goal:** The player. The client moves, looks, and interacts: the vanilla movement model with its recorded test vectors, an input path from the window into the session, the camera's full FOV formula with view bobbing, a gamemode-dependent block raycast, break and place with client-side prediction over the two block-change packets, the crack overlay and the block outline, the movement packets a server expects, and death and respawn that leaves no stale state.

**Architecture:** The session thread also becomes the tick thread: a fixed 20 Hz step inside its play loop drives the player's state — physics, prediction, break progress, and the clock and cloud counters — while the read loop's discipline holds (ticks are bounded; a readable frame is always read before mesh or tick work). The window forwards key edges and mouse deltas into the session over one channel and receives per-tick player state, aim, health and break events back over the existing event channel. `oxide-game` grows the player, physics, input, and interaction modules; `oxide-world` grows collision, hardness, and the mutable block write with its light hooks; `oxide-proto-v47` grows the movement, digging, placement, and block-change packets; `oxide-render` grows the camera's formula set and the world-overlay pass; `oxide-client` grows the capture rules, the input wiring, and the interim death view.

**Tech Stack:** Rust 2024 edition (rust-version 1.85, developed on 1.99), the M2 stack unchanged. **No new dependencies in this milestone**; no new crate edges — every task stays inside the section 5.1 table.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/oxidecraft-v1-design.md` (v6). M3 is the section 13 row "Player". Read section 11.1 before Tasks 1–10, section 6 before Task 1, the section 10 camera bullet and `docs/research/render-parity-survey.md` §5.1–5.5 before Tasks 2, 3 and 11, and `docs/research/protocol-47-reference.md` §2 (the movement, digging, placement and block-change rows and §2.3's ordering notes) and §3.3 before Tasks 5–10. Vanilla citations are files under `refs/_src/MCP-919` (uncommitted), the same source the research reports cite. The M2 final review's "what will fight M3" list (`refs/m2-final-review/review-report.md` §2) is this plan's standing risk list, and each item is answered by a task below.

## Decisions taken in this plan

| # | Decision | Rationale |

| 1 | **The tick runs on the session thread.** A fixed 20 Hz step scheduled inside the play loop (deadline-checked after every packet and every idle wait, with a bounded catch-up cap derived from the source's `Timer.updateTimer`). The session thread is the single owner of the world and the connection; a separate tick thread would need cross-thread world mutation and would put the read-loop discipline at risk. | 2 | **One per-tick event carries the player.** `ClientEvent::PlayerTick` (pose, ground and movement flags, tick count, a snapped marker) is emitted every tick and replaces `PlayerPosition`; a server correction (clientbound 0x08) applies to the player state, answers 0x06, and emits an immediate snapped tick. **Files:**

- Create: `crates/oxide-game/src/player.rs` — the player state.

- Produces:
